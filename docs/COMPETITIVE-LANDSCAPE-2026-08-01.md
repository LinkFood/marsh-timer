## 1. THE ANSWER, FIRST

**Nobody has built this.** Not one of the ~45 products examined scored above 7/12, no product scored above 1 on gate coverage, and no product in any category — hunting, fishing, weather, radar, or marine — assembles more than three of the six gates. But the flattering read stops there, because two things are closer than comfortable. First, **the closest thing to your doctrine is not an app at all** — it is a Ghost blog by an anonymous data engineer, [outdoorstats.com](https://web.archive.org/web/20260607050727/https://outdoorstats.com/best-days-to-hunt-california-waterfowl-refuges/), which ran 26,145 hunt-days of CDFW check-station records 2003–2026 and published ducks-per-hunter by day-of-week with stated denominators, minimum-N gating, Bonferroni correction, and headline nulls — the signature move, executed better than you have executed it, aimed at exactly the PLAN-mode question. It scored 7/12 and it is currently offline (Cloudflare 521, TCP timeout on 178.128.186.251, verified 2026-08-01) with no posts since March 2026. Second, **the closest competitor is a paper notebook**: waterfowlforum.net's Season Log threads show a hunter logging 12 consecutive seasons against a 20-field template including moon phase, wind velocity and cloud cover ([viewtopic.php?t=8426](https://waterfowlforum.net/viewtopic.php?t=8426), continuous back to 2014-15), and the $12 commercial hunting journals already encode all six of your gates as blank lines. The incumbent behavior has your exact ontology and zero data. Meanwhile, the single most dangerous real product — [FowlTracker](https://fowltracker.com/), NEXRAD roost detection from Maurice Pitesky's UC Davis avian-epidemiology lab, cost base paid by 1,500 poultry farms doing avian-flu biosecurity — goes nationwide in August 2026 and already ships a same-date-across-seasons historical view and a months-out "Best Windows to Hunt." It solves gate 1 better than you will, on somebody else's money, and it will be the thing every duck hunter compares your BIRDS gate against within one season.

## 3. WHAT IS ALREADY TAKEN

Buy, integrate, or skip. Do not build these.

1. **Offline mapping and navigation.** onX has solved it to a standard you will not match: region download with user-selectable resolution, full GPS/layers/tools in airplane mode, offline device-to-device waypoint sharing, CarPlay ([FAQ](https://www.onxmaps.com/hunt/app/faq)). Assume the hunter already carries onX. Position as "onX is the map, this is the decision."
2. **Wind-for-spread geometry.** Given away free by [ScoutLook SetZone](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.scoutlook.hunting) (7-day wind map for placing decoys), shipped by [HuntStand HuntZone](https://www.huntstand.com/huntstand-app/) (visual compass for setting spreads in the dark), and half-shipped by onX Optimal Wind. Wind-only geometry is a table stake, not a wedge. The *sun azimuth* half is still open — nobody computes it.
3. **NEXRAD roost detection as computer vision.** [darkecology/roostui](https://github.com/darkecology/roostui) is MIT-licensed and proven at 612,786 scans. [MistNet](https://doi.org/10.1111/2041-210X.13280) separates biology from precipitation at 95.9%/1.3%. Level-II is free on AWS Open Data. Do not write a detector.
4. **The counts-with-denominators primitive.** [xmACIS2 Calendar Day Frequencies](https://xmacis.rcc-acis.org/) has shipped "N times out of M non-missing years at this station" since 2006, over the free ACIS API. This is not your moat. A competent competitor hits that API in a weekend.
5. **Species ID from a photo.** [WaterfowlAI's WingID](https://www.waterfowlai.com/) is free at a claimed 96% confidence; [FowlTracker BirdID](https://app.fowltracker.com/docs/bird_id) does it in-app. Integrate or skip — but note both require a server, so neither works in a marsh.
6. **Waterfowl migration science.** The [Weather Severity Index](https://schummerlab.weebly.com/duck-migration-forecast.html) (Schummer 2010, JWM 74:94, departure threshold WSI≈7.5) is published, validated by independent telemetry (PMC8729067: snow days > frost days > temperature; wind, pressure, cloud and precip "received much lower support"), and the [Aagaard continental energetics model](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8853969/) — daily timestep, 62 years, R²=0.86 — is sitting in a journal supplement as an R file. Do not re-derive.
7. **The day-of-week pressure effect.** Outdoor Stats already measured it: Wed 1.99 and Sat 1.99 ducks/hunter vs Sun 1.40, 21 straight seasons of Saturday beating Sunday, over 26,145 hunt-days. Cite them, don't rebuild them.

## 4. WHAT IS GENUINELY OPEN — AND WHY

### Open because nobody built it (real lanes)

**PLAN mode past 15 days. Nothing, anywhere.** Ceiling of the entire market: HuntProof 14d, HuntWise 14–15d, HuntStand 15d (deer species only, waterfowl explicitly excluded), onX Wind Calendar 7d, ScoutLook 7d, FowlTracker's historical averages. Every one is a *weather* horizon, and a weather horizon structurally cannot exceed two weeks. The r/Waterfowl thread asking for a [7–10 day freeze-line forecast to "schedule my hunting trips"](https://www.reddit.com/r/Waterfowl/comments/18ohuj2/frostfreeze_line_map_extended_forecast/) got zero product answers, one raw NOAA URL, and one "Following." Meanwhile hunters already plan months out by hand — quota-hunt applications are submitted seasons ahead. **The lane is empty because every company here built a weather product. That is structural, not evidentiary.** This is your strongest opening.

**Tides in a waterfowl app. Zero products.** DU's own editorial says tide is the largest factor in coastal marsh hunting; DU's own app has no tides. Every article's advice is "go use a generic tide app." The [one claim](https://www.ducks.org/hunting/waterfowl-hunting-tips/waterfowlers-world-waterfowling-apps) that Duckr had tide tables (2021) is contradicted by Duckr's current site and App Store listing. Empty because the addressable subset is coastal — a real market limitation, not a technical one.

**The PRESSURE gate. Nobody serves it, and it is the best-evidenced gate you have.** Outdoor Stats' natural experiment: when Christmas falls on Saturday, California refuges close, and the following Sunday hits 2.49 DPH vs 2.09 for a normal December Saturday and 1.48 for a normal December Sunday — the gap *inverts* despite turnout rising 51→73 hunters/area. The effect orders monotonically by how much pressure was removed (Sat-closed +0.59 > Wed-closed +0.31 > Sun-closed +0.25) across 9 events / 253 area-days. Backed by GPS telemetry ([Movement Ecology 2025, s40462-025-00565-2](https://doi.org/10.1186/s40462-025-00565-2): ducks arrive later and leave earlier during hunting season). And Outdoor Stats named the acquisition route: CDFW harvest PDFs plus **reservation/draw records via public records request**, where applicant counts are a *leading* indicator available before the hunt day. Most states run comparable draw systems.

**The offline decision surface.** Every candidate scored 0–1 on axis B and the ones that scored well scored on *maps*. onX states outright that "wind data requires internet connectivity," and its weather "will not update while your mobile device is in airplane mode." Nobody ships an offline shooting-time countdown, offline tide-now, offline bag tracker, offline setup diagram. Empty because it is unglamorous engineering.

**A national assembly of state weekly aerial surveys.** Illinois INHS flies 23 Illinois River + 18 central Mississippi sites weekly; Ohio, Indiana, Louisiana, Mississippi and Delaware all publish counts (Delaware on an open-data portal). Migration Station has six states. Nobody has assembled them nationally, and this is the only sub-weekly gate-1 substrate that exists besides eBird and radar.

### Empty for a reason — read these twice

**GATE 4 (MOON) may not survive contact with the record.** No peer-reviewed waterfowl study testing moon phase × cloud cover against feeding duration or next-morning flight activity could be located. DU's own authoritative popular summary of nocturnal waterfowl behavior — ["Understanding Waterfowl: Night Moves"](https://www.ducks.org/conservation/waterfowl-research-science/understanding-waterfowl-night-moves) — does not mention moon phase, lunar illumination, or cloud cover anywhere; it attributes nocturnal patterns to site fidelity and crepuscular movement. The only direct empirical test of the solunar premise (Quigley, Gonzalez & Kauwe 2023: 361 fishing trips, 1,355 hours) found *none* of the seven most popular solunar services had any significant relationship to CPUE. Outdoor Stats independently ran 22 seasons of California data and titled the result ["The Full Moon Duck Hunting Myth"](https://web.archive.org/web/20260306055911/https://outdoorstats.com/) with the summary "the real signal isn't the moon." **Ship moon as a displayed measurement — here is the illumination, here is the cloud cover, we do not know what it does. Do not ship it as a gate that can zero a day. That is the one place this product would assert something the record does not support.**

**Radar birds for waterfowl is empty because it is hard, not because nobody thought of it.** Species discrimination from NEXRAD is flatly unsolved — BirdCast's own FAQ: "radar data cannot determine specific species in clouds of migrating birds." The load-bearing waterfowl paper ([Buler et al. 2012, PLoS ONE 7(7):e41571](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0041571)) did not identify waterfowl by signature — it sampled a known wintering wetland at sun elevation −5° and *assumed* waterfowl dominance; the famous "97% waterfowl" figure was imported from a Louisiana study and the authors explicitly declined to verify it for their own site. Correlation with radio-marked ground truth: r = 0.54–0.62. Resolution ~19 hectares, effective range ~83 km. Worse, the timing is inverted: roost *rings* are a dawn phenomenon (temperature-inversion beam bending), but the validated waterfowl work samples the *evening* exodus. And blackbird/starling/grackle roosts throw identical-looking dawn rings across exactly the Delta, Gulf Coast and Central Valley geographies during exactly duck season, with no published method for telling them apart. **An honest radar gate is buildable only as a heavily location-primed, heavily hedged measurement that refuses out loud when the confounder is plausible. Anyone selling "there are ducks here" from NEXRAD is lying.** This is research, not engineering. Budget accordingly.

**The six-gate conjunction itself is untested — unfalsified but unvalidated.** Both published hunter-success analyses are additive regressions. Gammonley 2022 (JWM 86) concluded uncontrolled factors dominate; the Illinois 1981–2000 analysis found four significant variables of thirteen and only one under management control; DU's own 2002–2017 flyway analysis found exactly one positive relationship. The literature's finding — that no single variable predicts hunt success — is *more consistent* with a conjunction than with a score, but that is an inference, not a result. Present six measurements with six denominators. Do not claim the AND is proven.

**FIELD mode was built and died.** MOJO Waterfowl OPS shipped offline nautical charts + legal-shooting-time alarm + duck ID in 2014 and cannot be found on a store today. That is a *market* signal.

**Willingness to pay is the most unvalidated thing in this entire document.** The North American Waterfowler ([App Store id1581268201](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1581268201)) has **37 lifetime ratings** after four years at $49.99/yr. BlindBuddy has **6 ratings** at $89.99/yr. The four bootstrapped waterfowl decision apps price at $10–30/yr. No VC has funded a waterfowl decision tool; the one funded waterfowl company (WaterfowlAI) is building club-ops SaaS with embedded payments. Nobody is coming for this with money — which also means nobody with money has validated that hunters will pay for it.

## 5. THE EDGE, HONESTLY ASSESSED

The four measured nulls are: (1) summing a conjunction destroys it; (2) precedent carries no forward information, −0.19pp over 1.35M paired days; (3) two measurement constructions cannot be ranked against each other, 43% band disagreement; (4) semantic similarity over environmental data finds nothing actionable.

**The case that they are worth nothing.** Negative knowledge does not ship features. A competitor does not need any of it — HuntWise has 2.5M+ downloads selling "the odds of seeing your target species," HuntProof sells "weeks before anyone else" at $29.99, Fishbrain leads a mature market with an undisclosed 1–10 score, and not one user in any forum thread found in this sweep has ever audited any of them. The market does not punish being wrong about this. Worse, **result (2) is a knife pointed at your own thesis**: if precedent carries no forward information, then "a tide this steep has happened 15 of the last 21 September 1sts at this station" is a *descriptive* object, not decision support. It tells the hunter what the record looks like; your own data says it does not tell him what tomorrow will do. If the pitch is "this count helps you choose," you are asserting something your own 1.35M paired days refuse. And these are sunk — the value already accrued as four things you did not build. They cannot be a moat because they cannot be defended and would not be copied.

**The case that they are worth something.** Look at the field: BlindBuddy, HuntProof, DuckSmart, Flyway Forecast, HuntWise, HuntStand, Fish & Tides, Fishbrain, FowlTracker's ordinal ladder — **every single product that touched analysis collapsed to a fused number.** That is not coincidence, it is a gravity well, and it pulls hardest at month nine when users start saying "just give me a number." Result (1) is the only thing that resists it. Result (2) is the more valuable one commercially, because it correctly *rescopes the product*: weather-precedent does not carry forward, but tide, moon, sun azimuth, season dates and day-of-week are **deterministic** — computable years out, not forecast at all. Result (2) is the reason PLAN mode should lead with the computables and treat weather-conditioned counts as context, not as the promise. That is a scoping decision with money attached, and it is the exact lane the sweep found empty. Result (3) is a shipping constraint that prevents a whole class of embarrassment: never rank two differently-constructed measurements against each other in the same UI, which kills any "score across gates" surface before it is drawn. Result (4) closes the semantic-retrieval branch permanently.

**Where this lands.** The nulls are worth a great deal to *your* decisions and approximately zero as a defensive asset. They bought you a correct repricing — from "prediction engine" to "measurement instrument plus a calendar computer that refuses to predict" — and that repricing points at the one genuinely open lane (PLAN, on deterministic inputs). They will not slow a competitor down by one day, because a competitor would simply ship the wrong thing and the market would not notice. **The honest summary: your edge is not knowledge, it is the willingness to build the boring deterministic half that nobody built because it is not a weather product.** And take result (2) seriously enough to change the copy: the counts are receipts and context, not forward guidance. If you sell them as forward guidance, you become the thing you built this to refuse.

**Internal flag, urgent:** marsh-timer's convergence engine currently weights "BirdCast — radar migration intensity" at 20 of 135. Per [BirdCast's own FAQ](https://birdcast.org/faq/), that variable measures nocturnal passerines outside waterfowl migration windows, and it is being *summed* into a fused score. Wrong taxon and wrong operation.

## 6. STEAL THIS

**UI / interaction**
1. **The compass sector picker** — onX Optimal Wind: tap a waypoint, eight compass wedges bloom, tap up to seven to declare the wind you want there ([tutorial](https://www.onxmaps.com/hunt/tutorials/optimal-wind)). One gesture, no typing, validated by 274K ratings. Steal the control; change the physics — theirs is deer-scent ("wind is carrying your scent away from the target area"), yours is birds-landing-into-wind plus sun-at-your-back.
2. **The Wind Calendar grid** — rows = days, columns = sunrise / midday / sunset, multiple saved blinds compared side by side. Hunters already read this fluently. onX can only fill 7 columns because wind is a forecast; tide, moon, sun and season dates fill 90. **Take their layout and extend the axis they cannot.** Replace green/yellow/red with the count.
3. **The date-by-season grid** — FowlTracker's [location historical view](https://app.fowltracker.com/docs/location_historical_data): calendar dates across, seasons down. Far better carrier for counts-with-denominators than any line chart — you read "N of the last M" straight off a column. Put integers and denominators in the cells instead of colors.
4. **"Best Windows to Hunt"** — FowlTracker's PLAN primitive: average each calendar day across all seasons on record, group consecutive strong days into a named window. Right shape, wrong arithmetic (they average an ordinal ladder). Same UI, honest engine.
5. **Scrubbable time axis over imagery** — onX [Recent Imagery](https://www.onxmaps.com/hunt/app/features/recent-imagery), biweekly 5m frames back to March 2023. Drag through time and *watch* water arrive. Pair each frame with the gauge/tide reading from that date so photo and number corroborate.
6. **The refusal state, already shipped** — onX renders the word "Offline" on a waypoint when the nearest station is unavailable rather than interpolating a plausible wind. Copy the pattern and the plainness of the word.

**Method**
7. **The natural-experiment method** — the best idea in the sweep. Outdoor Stats used mandatory refuge closures as a no-pressure treatment and got a *monotone dose-response gradient* by amount of pressure removed. That converts correlation to mechanism with no forward join and no prediction. Find the equivalents in your record: closures, season splits, ice-out, emergency closures, government shutdowns.
8. **Effect size in hunter units** — "16 rainy trips to accumulate one extra bird," "that's one-thirtieth of an average bag." Converts a statistically real effect into a sentence that correctly tells the hunter to ignore it. Best copywriting device in the category.
9. **Name the stratum aloud before the number** — "same refuge, same week, same season." The stratum *is* the receipt.
10. **Publish nulls as headlines** — Outdoor Stats' largest visual element is a giant "0" captioned "effect of temperature" (r = −0.003, p = 0.64), and it works. Proof the doctrine sells rather than a risk.
11. **Minimum-N gating as an editorial rule** — they only publish refuge-day combinations with 100+ hunt-days behind them. That is "refuses out loud when the record is too thin," already implemented. Also see [NOAA CO-OPS](https://tidesandcurrents.noaa.gov/est/): will not analyze a gauge under 30 years.

**Data model / schema**
12. **Split "Waterfowl Activity" from "Waterfowl Responsiveness"** — from the waterfowlforum 20-field template. Birds-present and birds-working are different diagnoses and no app distinguishes them.
13. **A confounder column** — that template has Malfunctions, Curses, Kudos. Without it, every bad morning is silently attributed to conditions and your denominators rot. Same class of bug as `feedback_null_coerced_to_zero_fabricates`: an unexplained zero is not a conditions zero.
14. **Make logging a skunk day one tap and the default path** — paper logs failure at the same cost as success; apps built around the grip-and-grin under-record it. Your denominator only exists if zeros are as cheap to record as limits.
15. **Site is the primary key, not date** — one book per hole, as the forum practice already does ("Location: #3").
16. **The decoy-spread drawing grid with a compass rose** (Etsy/Amazon paper logs) — let him draw and save what he actually set, then hand it back: "your spread from the last three NW-15 mornings in this hole."
17. **The crew is the unit** — onX collaborative folders and one-tap waypoint sharing; a real DU-featured club runs blinds, water-control structures, levee gates and pumps on it. Duck hunting is a crew sport and every solo-user app misreads that.

**Monetization**
18. **The free tier should be data-shaped, not time-shaped** — FowlTracker gives the full product surface but only the 1st of each month. The archive *is* the paywall. Correct geometry for a counts-against-history product.

**Bottom line, unsweetened:** the space is not crowded, but it is not empty either — it is *scattered*, and the scattering is the opportunity. Five of six gates are served somewhere by somebody, usually free, usually well. The two things nobody has are the deterministic planning horizon and the refusal to fuse, and only one of those is defensible. The four nulls repriced the product correctly but confer no moat. The largest unvalidated risk in this document is not a competitor — it is that the four bootstrapped waterfowl decision apps price at $10–30/yr and the best-funded one has 37 lifetime ratings.
