## 3. QUERY PATH — recommendation: hybrid, transposed column store

Three candidates evaluated against measured cost:

**(a) Baked cell table — rejected.** Ruling 1 makes the band a moving window around the live event's percentile, not a fixed tier. Keyed on the anchor byte that is 50 states × 2 sides × 366 doy × 255 bytes = **9.33M rows**, and every band change forces a re-bake — while the band is explicitly undecided until after the backfill.

**(b) Pure edge function — rejected.** Works (8.11 MB frame store loads in 1,078 ms over 28 parallel pages) but re-pulls the whole store per cold isolate. `hunt-atlas-spot` is the standing proof of where that ends: 1.98–28.43 s measured, a known product defect.

**(c) Hybrid — recommended.** One row per `(instrument_id, metric, side)` holding that slot's entire column as `bytea`, plus the day-index array once. **144 rows, ~4 MB total.** A card request is one PostgREST GET of the user's state column — ~56 KB — plus one `board_pool_luts` row (3 KB) for `invertPct` display. The counting engine is then a scan over ~1,600 bytes in the browser: microseconds.

Why this shape: baking the *answers* prices in a band decision that hasn't been made. Baking the *column* prices in nothing — any band, any window, any future rule, no re-bake. Only the current year appends daily, which `hunt-frame-daily` already has the layout loaded to do.

Table shape and grants copied verbatim from `supabase/migrations/20260717120000_planting_climatology.sql:37-40`.

**Liftable from `scripts/mine/`:** `stats.ts` entirely — `wilsonInterval:132`, `fisherExactOneSided:90`, `lift:208` have zero imports and run unmodified in browser or Deno. `frames.ts` `invertPct:231`, `doyOfIso:170` are pure. **Not liftable:** `bandStats:842` and `buildSentence:930` both take a `CellCtx` built from storm-outcome anchors — a different follow definition than the card's, and moot under Ruling 4 anyway. `mine.ts:32` imports `fs`.

## 4. LIVE SOURCES — verification results (§12.4)

**Open-Meteo — green on mechanics, one licensing flag.** The full 2° CONUS lattice (390 points) returns in **one request, 2.91 MB, 2.13 s**. Hard cap 1,000 locations/request; GET dies at ~500 on URL length (`HTTP 414`), so **use POST** — verified working. Critically: **each location counts as a call.** 390 points × 4 refreshes/day = 1,560 calls/day against a 10,000/day free ceiling (16%); land-masking to ~230 points drops it to 920. Hourly refresh would consume 94% of the daily and 95% of the monthly cap — do not.

⚠️ **Two flags.** (1) Free tier is **non-commercial only**; data is CC-BY 4.0 and requires attribution on the map. If duckcountdown.com is ever monetized, the field layer needs a paid key. (2) **UNVERIFIED** — current total DCD Open-Meteo consumption across `hunt-weather-watchdog`, `hunt-air-quality`, `hunt-soil-monitor`, `hunt-river-discharge` and `hunt-alerts` was never measured. The lattice budget must be added on top of that baseline, not sized against a clean 10k. Measure in week one.

**WPC coded surface bulletin — ALIVE.** Both resolutions publish and are current: `wpc.ncep.noaa.gov/discussions/codsus` and `.../codsus_hr` (use the high-res). Verified live, `last-modified` matching the 21Z run. Vocabulary confirmed as the spec assumed: `HIGHS`, `LOWS`, `TROF`, `COLD`, `WARM`, `STNRY`, `OCFNT`, each with a strength token. **Cadence 3-hourly** (00/03/06/09/12/15/18/21Z); poll every 30 min against `max-age=1800`, dedupe on the `VALID` line. Coded text history 2003→present exists on Zenodo if backfill is ever wanted.

**Consequence: Ruling 7's gradient fallback is not needed for v1b.** Draw NWS's analyzed fronts with standard symbology, attributed. Keep the fallback coded as the failure path only.

**IEM NEXRAD — free, public domain, but Albers is not served.** Advertised CRS list is complete and short: `EPSG:102100, 26915, 3857, 4326, 900913`. `EPSG:5070` and `ESRI:102003` both return `InvalidSRS` — confirmed by GetMap, not just capabilities. Terms are public domain, attribution appreciated, no documented rate limit.

**This is cheap, not incompatible.** `EPSG:4326` is honored, and equirectangular is the easiest possible warp source. One `GetMap` at the board's aspect, warped through an inverse-Albers LUT — **not** a tile pyramid, since every tile layer is `-900913` (Mercator) and would need stitching *and* warping. The XYZ-tiles line in spec §5.1 is the wrong integration and should be struck.

## 5. RENDERER — 2.5 weeks, and the projection fork is decided

Recon block C independently re-verified: `boardPlayer.ts` is 641 lines, `drawFrame:568-600` is a fixed 4-stage pipeline, and grep for `save()|restore()|rotate(|clip(|createPattern|drawImage|globalAlpha` returns **zero hits**.

**`src/lib/isobars.ts` is dead** — deleted 2026-07-02 in `53ab343` along with Mapbox, and its `turf` dependency is gone from `package.json`. Its marching-squares cell loop (~60 dependency-free lines at `git show 53ab343^:src/lib/isobars.ts`) is salvageable, but it emits unstitched 2-point segments and its saddle case is push-order dependent. **Recommend skipping isolines entirely** — per Ruling 2 the field is navigation, not measurement, so a shaded field needs no contour lines and no labels.

| Field (lattice → RGBA in lat/lon → warp → blit) | ~185 | ~15 |
| Fronts, WPC symbology (arc-length walk, tangent glyphs) | ~140 | 0 |

**The load-bearing efficiency: one inverse-Albers LUT serves both the field and the radar.** Benchmarked, not guessed — LUT build 32 ms for 594,750 px, gather <1 ms, 2.38 MB resident, invariant under resize because it lives in the fixed 975×610 projection space. Round-trip error 2.0e-3, entirely from the existing 0.1 rounding in `project():62`.

**The layer contract: do not touch `BoardFilm`.** It is the wire format of two frozen shipped artifacts (`public/board/uri-2021.json`, `sandy-2012.json`). Add an optional fifth parameter instead — `drawFrame(ctx, model, t, nowMs, overlays?)` — and all four existing call sites compile untouched and take the identical path when it is `undefined`. The one real edit: `drawGround:351-365` splits into fill and borders, because correct z-order puts the borders back **on top** of a shaded field.

**Projection fork — Option A, stay Albers, decisively.** Cost is the LUT plus moving `project()` from build-time to `src/lib/` (~5 lines plus import fixes). Option B (Mercator) saves ~90 lines of warp and costs 5–7 days: both geometry files re-baked with a generator **that does not exist in the repo** (`d3-geo` is not installed; both files landed as committed artifacts), `PROJ_VERSION` bumped, the `ghcn-tx = 461.1,442.9` regression anchor killed, and **AK/HI has no answer** — Mercator has no inset convention and stretches Anchorage ~2× against the Gulf. Albers-with-insets is standard precisely because of this. Mercator also puts three shipped surfaces into re-verification six weeks from the opener.

**Area hit-testing:** lift `pointInState`/`hitState` from `AtlasPage.tsx:82-114` — even-odd point-in-polygon with a bbox pre-filter, already proven at interactive rates in this exact coordinate space. ~75 lines. Not a colour-key canvas (the board breathes — `drawDot`'s glow is a function of `nowMs`), not an SVG overlay (ships the rings twice; `/atlas` already found manual hit-testing faster).

**§8.1 projection registration is fixed under either option**, into `albers_x_v2`/`albers_y_v2` per Ruling 4 of Amendment 1.1 — never overwritten. Independently reproduced: 31 of 50 dots outside their own state's bbox (`CT` dx −113, `CA` dx +74). `board_instruments` is 72 rows; reprojecting is minutes of compute.

## 8. WHAT I EXPECT TO SLIP

Two answers, because the honest ones are different questions.

**Most likely to slip: fronts (v1b).** It is the only map layer with no fixture corpus, and the cost is not the tangent math — it is the parse, the cadence, and visual QA that pips land on the correct side of the line. That is a correctness claim made in NWS's own visual language, so it has to be right. **Low consequence under this sequencing**: v1a ships ~Aug 15 and does not depend on it. Second most likely: hover, because it forces the `<BoardCanvas>` dedupe that recon C1 deferred (the ~30-line pointer handler is copy-pasted between `BoardPage.tsx:256-284` and `TodayPage.tsx:391-422`).

**Most damaging if it slips: season dates — and the risk is external.** There is **no 2026-27 federal frameworks rule**. FWS replaced annual promulgation with a 3-year memorandum (FR 2026-12951, published 2026-06-26, effective 2026-08-25); the 2026-27 memorandum is due **"no later than August 31"** with a 30-day comment period. States are publishing now — Alabama and Pennsylvania have 2026-27 dates live today, ~29 states verified by one aggregator as of 07-17 — but a slice of them are **provisional until the same week we ship**, and at least one state (NC) says so explicitly.

The countdown is the promise in the domain name and the one number a hunter checks in half a second. Mitigations, all in week one: store `source_url` + `fetched_at` per row, build the diff tooling immediately so the September re-verify is minutes rather than hours, and label provisional dates as provisional on the card rather than discovering the problem after the opener.

## 9. WHERE I STILL DISAGREE, OR NEED A RULING

**9.1 — ERA5 at a centroid is not "Maryland statewide."** The card's honesty line says *Maryland statewide. Not your marsh.* But an ERA5 pull at a state centroid is a **single grid point**, which is *less* than statewide — the label would overclaim in the opposite direction from the one we've been guarding against. Two options: relabel to something like *measured at a single point in central Maryland*, or sample 3–5 grid points per state and average. The second is nearly free — bulk coordinates are already proven, so 50 states × 5 points is 250 locations, one request — and it makes the number a genuine areal aggregate without reintroducing the station-network artifact that ERA5 was chosen to escape. **Recommend the 5-point average.** Needs your ruling before the backfill runs, because it changes what gets stored.

**9.2 — the decade distribution has a ragged last bar.** 1979–2026 gives 4 full decades and two partial ones (the 1970s contributes one year, the 2020s six). The 2020s bar will read as a collapse partly because it is 60% of a decade. Either normalize to per-year rates (and say so), or drop partial decades from the display. This is exactly the kind of "true number, misleading picture" the card exists to avoid.

**9.3 — spec §5.1's "radar consumed as tiles" should be struck**, replaced with a single `EPSG:4326` GetMap. Evidence in §4 above.

**9.4 — noted for the record, per Ruling 3a's closing instruction.** Temperature *level* is the only variable measured to carry a real forward signal: **1.42–1.66× lift over the unconditional base rate after run-correction, up to 3.04× at a 0.5% band.** Delta counts cleanly and predicts nothing (0.91–1.07×). Under Ruling 4 that is irrelevant to v1 — but it is the strongest empirical result this project has produced and it is the reason the cold-snap card is worth building later. Recorded here so it is not lost.
