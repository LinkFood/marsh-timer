## 2. WHAT WE FOUND

More than we built. Every one of these was **already live**.

### Security — three holes, all closed
1. **248 tables carried anon `TRUNCATE`**, including `hunt_knowledge` (10M rows, the whole archive). TRUNCATE bypasses RLS, so every "RLS on, read-only policy" table was destroyable in one statement by a key that ships in three browser bundles. Closed cluster-wide, **then at the schema default ACL** — because creating two tables minutes later reopened it, proving point-in-time revokes decay.
2. **`hunt-embed-interaction` was an unauthenticated service-role write into the archive.** Anyone could POST arbitrary text under any `content_type` and have it embedded. Worse than the destructive hole: destruction is loud and restores from backup, this **fabricates silently** and vector search then serves it as an observation. Whitelisted to two interaction types.
3. **`lupa_designs` leaked to anon** in full, with DELETE/UPDATE/TRUNCATE. Closed.

### Fabrication — three families, ~2,400 rows
1. **1,095 fabricated weather events.** `?? 0` on Open-Meteo's trailing null forecast day invented a full slate every run at severity `high` — pressure "falls" to vacuum, first freezes in **July**. Faucet fixed, rows marked.
2. **1,257 fabricated brain vectors** — the same events embedded as `weather-event` rows, retrieved by vector search as observations, and rendered as live chips on `/atlas`. Marked and **unembedded**.
3. **19 physically impossible readings** in 9.7M, including Colorado at **−474 °F**, below absolute zero. Ignorable in most products, fatal in one that counts *tails* — a sentinel is by construction the most extreme member.

### Correctness
- **`/atlas` was sign-inverted** — showing New York at +1.7σ **hot** on a day the frame store had it at cold depth 1.000. Cause: reading 2025-07-25 because `ghcn-daily` ends 2025-12-31.
- **`bake-luts` could not run at all** — cache-envelope mismatch, silently blocking week two.
- **`npm test` could never run** — `setupFiles` pointed at a file that never existed. No `src/` test had ever executed. Now **76 passing.**
- **The board paired contradictions** — *"Nebraska — colder than 99% · Extreme Heat Watch"*. No direction check on corroboration, and the alert was chosen by count *before* direction was known, which is how California's Extreme Heat Warning lost to two Fire Weather Watches.
- **Ungraded forward rate on the front door** — *"of the last 12 days a watch stood here, 10 saw a Flood Warning within 3 days."* n=12, banned by Ruling 4.
- **The restore drill's own machinery lost data twice.** First `$n::bytea` double-encoding — row count and contiguity both passed on **100% corrupt blobs**. Then a DDL parser that returned at the first `CREATE TABLE`, silently dropping values for every column added by a later `ALTER` — already live on `board_pool_luts`'s entire frequency dictionary, all 8 `hunt_seasons` opener columns, and `planting_climatology.receipts`.

### The finding that changed the product
**The rarity map compared two different measurements.** Day-0 was one Open-Meteo forecast point at the state centroid; the pool was a GHCN station-network mean (MD 39, AK 362, TX 424 stations).

Measured over 45,059 state-days: **43.0% land in a different shade band, 18.1% flip hot vs cold.** One percentile point at the real band edges is **0.2–0.4 °F**. A per-state offset fitted leave-one-day-out moved band disagreement **30.61% → 30.61%** — nothing to correct, because it is not a bias.

Per the ruling, the depth claim is **withheld** behind `TAIL_DEPTH_IS_COMPARABLE = false` — one file, four named reopening conditions, restoring four surfaces in one edit.

**This was my error.** I gated the *forward* map on a bias measurement and shipped the *present* one without the same check.
